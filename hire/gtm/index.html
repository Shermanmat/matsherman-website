What I've built

I've founded and run companies end to end, not just consulted on them. I founded PubLoft and Seedscout, and worked at Product Hunt — all community-driven products where growth came from relationships, not ad spend. Today I run MatCap, an advisory-first venture firm where sourcing founders is itself a GTM problem: finding the right people before anyone else does, and earning enough trust that they want to work with us.

Node stacking — my approach

"Node stacking" is the framework I teach in my book, The Access Game: turn every contact into a node, make each one compound, and build a media asset that keeps generating warm access long after a single conversation ends. My podcast, Forward Thinking Founders, is that framework applied for six years straight — nearly 1,000 recorded conversations with founders, including the teams behind Mercury, Deel, Turing, Cognition, Alpaca, Varda, and Rainmaker, often reached at the pre-seed stage before anyone else was paying attention.

I've also done this offline: I helped bring Y Combinator's programs to Arizona, built relationships with people like Jason Calacanis and Eric Ries from a standing start in Phoenix, and now speak and run workshops on exactly this — see Speaking.
