What it's about

I grew up in Phoenix, nowhere near Silicon Valley, and had to figure out — the slow, expensive way — how founders actually get access to investors. The Access Game is the book I wish someone had handed me: part memoir of how an outsider found his way into Y Combinator's orbit, landed Jason Calacanis as a first investor, and built relationships with people like Eric Ries, and part playbook for any founder who wants to raise venture capital without already being in the room.

I wrote it for a selfish reason: many founders want to work with me but don't have the context on how this world works. Now I can hand them the book, and they're set. If millions more read it and benefit from knowing this stuff, all the better.

About the author

Mat Sherman is the founder of MatCap, an advisory-first, pre-seed venture firm. He's spent more than a decade in the startup ecosystem, and since 2020 has interviewed nearly 1,000 founders on his podcast, Forward Thinking Founders. A Phoenix-area native, he lives in Arizona with his wife and kids.
