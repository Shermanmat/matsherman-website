Track record

I'm the founder of MatCap, a venture advisory firm. Most investors back 10 to 30 companies and hope a few win; we support hundreds, take direct equity from almost day one, and stay involved as a partner throughout a company's life.

Since 2020, I've interviewed nearly 1,000 founders on my podcast, Forward Thinking Founders — including the founders of Mercury, Deel, Turing, Alpaca, Cognition, Varda, and Rainmaker, often at the pre-seed stage, well before they were an obvious bet.

How I evaluate founders

Those conversations are what let me pattern match at MatCap — nearly 1,000 interviews worth of training data to tell whether someone has the potential to be great, long before the metrics would tell you so. I wrote up the full thesis, and the access problem underneath it, in The Access Game, launching early 2027.
