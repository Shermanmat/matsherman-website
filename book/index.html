What it's about

I grew up in Phoenix, nowhere near Silicon Valley, and had to figure out — the slow, expensive way — how founders actually get access to investors. The Access Game is the book I wish someone had handed me: part memoir of how an outsider found his way into Y Combinator's orbit, landed Jason Calacanis as a first investor, and built relationships with people like Eric Ries, and part playbook for any founder who wants to raise venture capital without already being in the room.

I wrote it for a selfish reason: many founders want to work with me but don't have the context on how this world works. Now I can hand them the book, and they're set.

About the author

Mat Sherman is the founder and general partner of MatCap, an advisory-first pre-seed venture firm. He has spent more than a decade in the startup ecosystem, and since 2020 has worked to bring the advantages of the Bay Area to founders everywhere else.

He has made thousands of founder and investor introductions and works with more than 100 funds. Before MatCap he founded PubLoft and Seedscout, and worked at Product Hunt. He hosts the Forward Thinking Founders podcast.

A Phoenix-area native and Arizona State graduate, he lives in Scottsdale, Arizona.
