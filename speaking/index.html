What this looks like

  • Keynote / talk

    A single session, 30–60 minutes plus Q&A. Good for conferences, demo days, and company all-hands.

  • Workshop with founders

    A hands-on session where founders work through their own fundraising or growth problems in the room, not just listen.

  • Hands-on practice & consulting

    Deeper, ongoing work — reviewing pitches, running mock investor meetings, and working through what's actually blocking a raise.

Topics

  • Building AI-powered products

    Practical lessons from shipping AI tools for founders and investors — not the hype, the actual build.

  • Founding outside the coasts

    What building and investing looks like away from the Bay Area and NYC — the tradeoffs, and the advantages nobody talks about.
